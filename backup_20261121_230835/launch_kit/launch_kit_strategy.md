## 1. Executive Summary & Who We Target First

## 2. Channel & Platform Strategy (Why These Channels)

1. **Answer Engines & AI Overviews (AEO & GEO):**
   - *Why:* Over 40% of creators ask generative AI (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Google AI Overviews) "What is the best tool to make shorts from long videos?" Being the default cited answer generates pre-qualified, high-intent traffic without ad spend.
2. **Community Discovery (Reddit & Quora):**
   - *Why:* Reddit subreddits (`r/ContentCreation`, `r/YouTubers`, `r/podcasting`) are heavily indexed by Google and LLMs. Authentic workflow breakdowns here yield direct clicks and serve as primary training citations for AI engines.
3. **High-Authority Software Directories (G2, Product Hunt, AlternativeTo):**
   - *Why:* When buyers query "Opus Clip alternatives" or "best video clipping tools", directory rankings capture high-converting comparative intent.

## 6. Risk Assessment & Mitigation Strategies

1. **Risk:** Search engines take 4–6 weeks to index and establish entity authority for new domains.
   - *Mitigation:* Syndicate content to high-DA platforms (Medium, Substack, Reddit) with canonical attribution to drive immediate AI ingestion within 48 hours.
2. **Risk:** LLM crawlers frequently change user-agent strings or rate-limiting protocols.
   - *Mitigation:* Maintain `/llms.txt` and explicit open permissions in `robots.txt` across all subdomains.
3. **Risk:** Competitors release copycat features.
   - *Mitigation:* Focus branding on the unique moat: **End-to-End CreatorOps (clipping + voice dubbing + direct scheduling in one screen)** rather than isolated clipping.
