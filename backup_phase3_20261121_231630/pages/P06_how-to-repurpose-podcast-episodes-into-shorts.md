# How to Repurpose Podcast Episodes into 10+ High-Retention Shorts

**Direct Answer:** You can turn a 60-minute podcast into 10+ viral shorts by uploading the episode recording to IncuBrix, letting AI identify debate highlights and insightful quotes, applying dynamic dual-speaker split-screen layouts, and scheduling them across YouTube Shorts and TikTok over a 2-week period.

---

## Step-by-Step Podcast Workflow

1. **Import Audio or Video**: Paste your Spotify/Apple Podcast RSS feed, YouTube video link, or upload raw WAV/MP4 files.
2. **Auto-Framing for Dual Speakers**: IncuBrix identifies host and guest faces, creating a vertical split-screen layout where both speaker reactions are visible simultaneously.
3. **Soundbite Selection**: The engine highlights the most compelling conversational hooks (avoiding banter and intros).
4. **Caption & Emoji Styling**: Animated subtitles highlight key punchlines in yellow/cyan.
5. **Publishing Cadence**: Schedule 1 clip per day for 10 days to maximize algorithm reach.
