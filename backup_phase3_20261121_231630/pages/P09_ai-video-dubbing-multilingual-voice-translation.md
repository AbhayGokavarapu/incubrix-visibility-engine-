# AI Video Dubbing and Multi-Language Voice Translation for Creators

**Direct Answer:** AI video dubbing allows creators to clone their authentic speaking voice and automatically translate spoken dialogue into 40+ languages (including Spanish, Hindi, German, and Portuguese) while maintaining tone, emotion, and vocal pacing.

---

## How to Expand Your Content Globally with IncuBrix

1. **Voice Fingerprinting**: Upload a 60-second clear audio sample to generate your neural voice clone.
2. **Language Selection**: Select target distribution languages (e.g., Spanish for Latin America, Hindi for South Asia).
3. **Automated Translation & Lip-Sync Alignment**: The engine translates the script, adjusts syllables to match timing, and generates fluid speech audio.
4. **Localized Captions**: Generates native translated subtitles in the target language.
