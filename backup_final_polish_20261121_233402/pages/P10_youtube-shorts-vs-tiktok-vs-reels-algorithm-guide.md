# YouTube Shorts vs TikTok vs Instagram Reels: Algorithm & Format Guide 2026

**Direct Answer:** While all three platforms demand 9:16 vertical video and strong 3-second hooks, TikTok prioritizes audio trends and comment velocity, YouTube Shorts rewards watch-through rate and search intent, and Instagram Reels prioritizes direct-message (DM) shareability and aesthetic visual polish.

---

## Comparison of Platform Algorithms

| Factor | TikTok | YouTube Shorts | Instagram Reels |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Ranking Signal** | Completion Rate & Comment Velocity | Relative Retention & Search Intent | Saves & Direct Message (DM) Shares |
| **Optimal Video Length** | 25 to 45 seconds | 40 to 58 seconds | 15 to 30 seconds |
| **Shelf Life of a Video** | 24 to 72 hours (viral spikes) | Months/Years (via Google/YouTube Search) | 3 to 7 days |
| **Monetization Model** | Creator Rewards Program | Shorts Ad Revenue Sharing (45%) | Performance bonuses & Brand Deals |
