**Direct Answer:** To publish video clips to TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels, and LinkedIn simultaneously, connect your social accounts inside the IncuBrix dashboard, select your generated clips, set your release calendar, and enable automated posting with platform-optimized titles, hashtags, and thumbnail frames.

## Why Direct Social Auto-Publishing Matters
Creators who rely on manual mobile uploads report spending 30 to 45 minutes every single day just downloading files, logging into different apps, copy-pasting captions, and choosing sound settings.

IncuBrix connects directly via official developer partner APIs:
1. **TikTok Content Posting API**: Directly uploads high-bitrate vertical video.
2. **YouTube Data API**: Schedules public, unlisted, or private Shorts with SEO tags.
3. **Instagram Graph API**: Publishes directly to Instagram Reels with custom covers.
4. **LinkedIn Marketing API**: Posts video natively to personal profiles and company pages.
